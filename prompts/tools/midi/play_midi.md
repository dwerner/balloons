### play_midi

Play musical notes through the browser using Web Audio synthesis.

**Notation:**
- Notes: `C4`, `D#5`, `Eb3` (note + octave)
- Durations: `:w` whole, `:h` half, `:q` quarter (default), `:e` eighth, `:s` sixteenth
- Rests: `R`
- Chords: `[C4,E4,G4]`

**Example:** `"C4 D4 E4:h [C4,E4,G4]:w"` plays C-D-E(half)-Cmaj(whole)
